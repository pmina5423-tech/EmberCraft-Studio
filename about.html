Our Story

EmberCraft Studio began three years ago as a small weekend project, born from a passion for hand-poured candles and their ability to transform a space. What started with a handful of candles sold at local markets has since grown into a dedicated studio offering both a curated candle range and hands-on scent-blending workshops. Every candle remains hand-poured and small-batch, staying true to the studio's original values even as the business has grown.

See the candle range or book a workshop.

Mission & Vision

Our mission is to craft candles of genuine quality, using ingredients we stand behind, while creating an experience that invites customers to slow down and engage with the process of scent creation.

Our vision is to become Pretoria's leading destination for artisanal candles — recognised not simply as a product supplier, but as a studio offering a distinctive, memorable experience.

Meet the Maker

EmberCraft Studio remains a predominantly one-person operation, with its founder overseeing every stage of production — from pouring and scent blending to hosting workshops. This hands-on approach ensures consistent quality across every batch produced.
